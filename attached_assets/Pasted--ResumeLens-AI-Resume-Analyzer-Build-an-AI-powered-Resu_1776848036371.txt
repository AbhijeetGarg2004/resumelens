# ResumeLens - AI Resume Analyzer

Build an AI-powered Resume Analyzer using Replit that reviews resumes and sends detailed feedback via email.

This app allows users to upload their resume (PDF) and select the job role they are applying for.
Based on the selected role, AI analyzes the resume, scores it, and generates improvement suggestions.
If no role is selected, AI will intelligently evaluate the resume using its own understanding.

---

## Features

---

### 1. Resume Upload
- Upload resume as a **PDF file**
- Automatically extract resume content for AI analysis

---

### 2. Role-Based Evaluation
- Select job role from a dropdown:
  - Backend Developer
  - Frontend Developer
  - HR / Human Resources
  - Other (with custom input field)
- If no role is selected, AI evaluates the resume using **general best practices**

---

### 3. AI Resume Analysis
- Resume score (**out of 100**)
- Strengths of the resume
- Missing skills or sections
- Suggestions to improve content, structure, and keywords
- **ATS-friendly feedback**

---

### 4. Processing Status & User Experience
- Show a **loading screen or progress indicator** while AI is analyzing the resume
- Display friendly messages such as:
  - "Analyzing your resume..."
  - "Scoring your resume for the selected role..."
- Once processing is complete:
  - Instantly show the **resume score in the UI**
  - Display a success message confirming email delivery

---

### 5. Email Report Automation
- Generate a **clean, professional resume analysis report** using AI
- Automatically send the complete report via **Gmail**
- Email includes:
  - Resume score
  - Role-based feedback
  - Strengths and improvement areas
  - Actionable improvement tips
  - Final recommendation
- UI confirmation message:
  - "📩 Your detailed resume report has been sent to your email successfully."

---

## Tech Stack
- Replit (Full-stack app)
- PDF File Upload
- LLM (Gemini)
- Gmail integration for sending reports

---

## Target Users
- Students
- Job seekers
- Freshers
- Developers preparing for interviews

---

## Goal
Help users understand how strong their resume is for a specific job role, see their score instantly, and receive a detailed AI-generated improvement report via email.
